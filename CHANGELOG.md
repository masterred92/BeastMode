# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release for WoW: Forever (Interface 16001, client 1.60.1).
- Pet name, family and happiness (green / yellow / red), next to Blizzard's pet frame, never replacing it.
- Flash and count for every pet ability (with the ability's name) and for Summon Hawk (all ranks), per fight and session.
- After each fight: Blizzard's own damage-meter split of you, your pet and your Hawk, for the fight and the session, or "counting pet casts only".
- Green-and-leather box with talent-tree accents. A hawk screech on Summon Hawk, on every pet ability, or off. Built-in game sounds only.
- Movable, resizable and lockable box (240x150 below the middle of the screen), only shown on Hunters. `/bmode debug`, `/bmode meter`, `/bmode report`.
